## What shipped

Two event shapes (both `MotiveType.Money`), in `SocialEventType`:
- **`Eviction`** (flagship, multi-suspect): a landlord is killed; the tenants they're clearing out for
  a **redevelopment** are the suspects. `a` = landlord (victim), `group` = tenants. Layoffs-shaped.
- **`RentArrears`** (one-to-one): a non-paying tenant is killed; their landlord is the lone suspect.
  `a` = tenant (victim), `b` = landlord. Mostly enriches a tenant's MIXED pool (see "selector-starved").

**Clues** (custom-doc engine, follow the V2.2 pointer/detail split):
- Eviction → a **Redevelopment Plan** at the *landlord's home* naming the **PROPERTY ADDRESSES** being
  cleared (each tenant's unit) as clickable location links + board connections, signed by the landlord.
  (A property schedule, deliberately NOT a tenant name-list — the user's call. Addresses lead the player
  to the buildings → residents.)
- RentArrears → a **Rent Demand Notice** at the tenant-victim's home, naming/printing the landlord.

**Testimony** (pointer, no specifics): "they're a landlord, clearing one of their buildings for a
redevelopment" / "they'd fallen behind on rent, the landlord was chasing" — never names a specific flat
or other tenants (`SocialEvent.TestimonyAbout`). Affairs untouched.

## Hard-won API facts (all cost a round or more this session — DON'T re-learn them)

- **`NewBuilding.owner` DOES NOT EXIST.** The decompiled `owner` is a lambda-closure field
  (`__c__DisplayClass129_0`), not a building property. The landlord is `Human.GetLandlord()` (a real
  public method), resolved **per resident**. `PropertySim` groups residents by `home.building.buildingID`
  and takes the shared landlord.
- **No rent/lease/arrears is modelled** (`rentCost` was a false-positive substring; only an
  `evictionNotice` flag exists). The dispute is fully mod-synthesized over the real landlord relationship —
  exactly how workplace layers synthesized promotions over real `job` edges.
- **Name-knowledge is NOT `knowsName` and NOT `Acquaintance.dataKeys`.** Both are unpopulated for
  NPC↔NPC edges (`knowsName` ConnectionType appears once in the whole assembly = the enum line;
  `dataKeys` is empty even on a 0.83-known friend edge — it's player-centric). The real signal is a
  **DIRECTED** `Acquaintance` edge `A→B` with `known >= Motive.NameKnownThreshold` (default 0.35, config
  `[Selection] NameKnownThreshold`). Live data: real relationships (friend/neighbor) sit ~0.6–0.9; casual
  familiar-residence ~0.1–0.2. **Direction is load-bearing:** a `B→A` edge alone (B faintly familiar with
  A) does NOT mean A knows B — checking both directions was the bug that listed non-knowers as "knowers".
- **Addresses are clickable board links:** `NewGameLocation.evidenceEntry : EvidenceLocation : Evidence`,
  so `Strings.AddOrGetLink(location.evidenceEntry, null)` mints a `<link>` exactly like a citizen;
  `NewAddress.CreateEvidence()` exists to force it. `AddLocationConnection` draws doc→location board lines.
- **There is no forceable "tell me your name" dialog.** Dialog-option clicks invoke the special-case
  HANDLERS directly (`DoYouKnowThisPerson` etc.), NOT `DialogController.ExecuteDialog` (its prefix never
  fires on a click). `SpecialCase.inputName` has no handler. So F8's name aid is a HUD readout
  (`DebugTools.TalkingToLabel`, "TALKING TO: Name#id"), not an in-world reveal.

## Accepted design notes
- **RentArrears is selector-starved by design** (one suspect), so it rarely stands alone; it adds one
  Money suspect to a tenant's mixed pool. F6=Money reliably forces **Eviction**, not RentArrears.
- `PropertySim.Alive()` filters `isDead` only (not age), matching `WorkplaceSim`; SoD has no minors, and
  the selector's `IsValidActor` re-filters — so no under-floor shrink in practice (review-verified).
